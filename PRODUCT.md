# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
Two audiences arrive at the README and landing page:
1. **EHS / operations leads** at organisations that run Mitti (formerly SafetyCulture): safety managers, HSE directors, site and operations managers. They use Claude Desktop or ChatGPT, not terminals. Job: get answers across inspections, actions, issues and credentials without exporting CSVs; prepare for audits; chase overdue work.
2. **Developers, consultants and partners** who integrate Mitti with AI clients (Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, VS Code) or BI tools (Power BI, Qlik). Job: install in a minute, trust the safety model, script workflows, export clean data.

## Product Purpose
An open-source MCP server that lets AI assistants read, analyse and (when allowed) act on a Mitti account: 125 tools, a local analytics cache, statistically honest analyses, generated reports and BI exports. Success: a read-only user gets a correct Monday safety pulse within five minutes of landing on the README, and numbers reconcile with the Mitti app.

## Brand Personality
Field-grade, trustworthy, plain-spoken. Like good site equipment: practical, robust, labelled clearly, nothing decorative. Confidence comes from showing real outputs (reports, tool results, the dry-run plan) and the safety model, not from adjectives. References: Linear's clarity and restraint; Raycast's large, crisp product shots; Stripe docs' tabbed install snippets.

## Anti-references
- Generic AI SaaS: purple gradients, glass cards, sparkle icons, "supercharge your workflow" copy.
- Mitti / SafetyCulture's own branding, colours, logos or visual identity (trademark and honesty: this is an independent project).
- Dark hacker / terminal-black theme.
- Corporate safety-poster clichés: clip-art hard hats, stock photos of workers, red/yellow hazard stripes.

## Design Principles
1. **Show the real output.** Every claim is backed by a genuine artefact from the demo organisation: a report, a tool result, a dry-run plan. Never mock a chat transcript.
2. **Safety model up front.** Read-only by default, two-step destructive changes and no telemetry are selling points, so they sit near the top, not in a footnote.
3. **Two doors.** Managers get "what you can ask" and report images; developers get one-line installs per client. Neither has to wade through the other's content.
4. **Plain language.** Literal verbs and nouns ("lists overdue actions by site"), no buzzwords, no em dashes.
5. **Independent and honest.** Clear "not affiliated" notice; untested clients marked untested.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG 2.2 AA: body contrast >= 4.5:1, focus states visible, keyboard-navigable tabs, alt text on every screenshot, `prefers-reduced-motion` honoured, colour never the only signal.
